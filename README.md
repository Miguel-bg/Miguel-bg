### [Readr](https://github.com/Miguel-bg/readr)
**A social reading app with a real page-flip experience.**

- 📖 **Page-flip reader** with a book-like page-turning effect.
- 📥 **Smart import** of EPUB, PDF and DOCX with metadata extraction.
- ✍️ **Create your own books** with custom chapters.
- 🔖 **Highlights and bookmarks** to save pages and notes.
- 🌍 **Social reading:** follow readers, rankings and activity.
- 🌐 **Multilingual:** Portuguese, English and Spanish.
- 🛠️ **Built with** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Cloudflare Workers and D1.
- 🔧 *Ongoing:* refining text formatting and reading performance, areas I identified as weak in the first version.
